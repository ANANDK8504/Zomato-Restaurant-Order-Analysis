# 📊 Zomato Restaurant and Order Analysis Dashboard

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze Zomato restaurant and order data. The dashboard provides insights into restaurant performance, customer behavior, pricing trends, and city-wise sales distribution to support data-driven decision making.

---

## 📌 Key Metrics
- Total Customers
- Total Restaurants
- Total Sales
- Average Rating
- Top Cuisine

---

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

### Orders by Price Range
This analysis shows the distribution of orders across Low, Medium, and High price ranges, helping understand customer spending behavior.

### Restaurants Count by City
Displays the total number of restaurants available in each city, highlighting market presence and city-wise restaurant density.

### Orders by City
Shows the total number of orders placed in each city, helping identify high-demand locations.

### Restaurants by Revenue
Ranks restaurants based on total revenue generated, identifying top-performing restaurants contributing the most to overall sales.

### Customers by Payment Method
Analyzes customer preferences based on different payment methods such as Cash, UPI, and Cards.

### Month-wise Total Sales by City
Tracks monthly sales trends across different cities, helping identify seasonal patterns and city-level growth.

### Total Cost of Top 5 Restaurants
Shows the total cost contribution of the top 5 restaurants, highlighting major revenue drivers.

---

## 🛠 Tools and Technologies Used
- Power BI – Dashboard creation and data visualization
- SQL – Data querying and transformation
- Microsoft Excel – Data cleaning and preprocessing

---

## 🎯 Business Insights
- Medium price range generates the highest number of orders
- Certain cities contribute significantly higher sales volume
- A small number of restaurants drive a major portion of revenue
- Digital payment methods are preferred by most customers
- Sales show noticeable variation across different months

---

## 📌 Conclusion
This dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of Zomato’s restaurant and order data. It helps stakeholders identify top-performing restaurants, understand customer behavior, optimize pricing strategies, and focus on high-growth cities.
